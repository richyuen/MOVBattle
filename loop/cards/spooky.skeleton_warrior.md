## Characteristics
The Skeleton Warrior is the basic melee unit of the Spooky faction, and the cheapest and weakest unit in the faction. They may spook enemy units, causing them to flee out of fright.
## Appearance
The Skeleton Warrior, unsurprisingly, is a Stiffy. It is dressed in ragged shorts, a Roman-style helmet and greaves. It wields a small shield and a sword. When it is killed, it collapses into bones. It makes rattling noises, and occasionally growls or roars in a wheezy, hollow voice.
