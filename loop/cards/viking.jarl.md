## Characteristics
The Jarl (Pronounced yarl) is the boss Unit of the Viking Faction. In the Open Alpha, they jumped at their enemies like Samurai Masters. In the Closed Alpha, they use their axes "properly" and no longer jump around. After being implemented into the new animation system, two versions of this unit were created, one which swings one axe (Axe) and one which spins two axes rapidly (Axe Master). Landfall put out a poll on Twitter asking which unit to keep. One axe won the vote; however, many people have replied saying they want both which sort of happened since the Chieftain does a similar thing. An article dealing with the other unit can be found here.
## Appearance
The Jarl is a mighty Wobbler that frolics into battle with a bearded Battle Axe. The most notable piece of clothing on the Jarl is their bear pelt. The pelt itself has googly eyes, brown fur, teeth, and claws present. The Jarl dawns a stereotypical Viking beard. Despite being shirtless, it wears arm wraps with fur and has puffy pants with leather snow boots. The other prominent item is the leather belt that dawns the Landfall logo, a theme this game’s Vikings do. The head of the Battle Axe wielded by the Jarl presumably appears to resemble bronze, a brown colored metal. In the Open and Closed Alphas, the Jarl (during which was simply called Axe) had their signature pelt, but had no clothes, let alone a beard. The pelt however had its eyes crossed.
