## Characteristics
The Cavalry is a Knight mounted on a Cavalry Horse. Now a Cavalry Knight, they are equipped with a Cavalry Spear to skewer any enemy they come into and a Cavalry Shield to protect them from small projectiles. The horse allows the Cavalry Knight ride swiftly into battle, tanking attacks from the enemy and trampling units that get into their way. Sharing the Jouster Horse's ability to create shockwaves wherever they canter, the Cavalry Horse can trample any enemy units that blocks their way. In addition, whoever is unfortunate enough to get under them can get kicked by the horse's hind legs, taking even more injuries. The horse's front legs on the other hand are harmless when struck. When engaging, the Cavalry Horse will pull back and forcefully charge into enemy forces, disrupting their formation and trampling the weak to their demise.
## Appearance
They appear to be a regular Knight unit, but with a spear. They also have a horse with a saddle blanket and some neck and head armor. The unit's spear appears to have a spear head with two smaller ones in it, and three flags hanging down from it.
