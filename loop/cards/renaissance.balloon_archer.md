## Characteristics
The Balloon Archer fires balloon arrows. The arrows have balloons attached to them that causes whatever unit the arrow has hit to float upward. Heavier units may require more balloons to float.
## Appearance
It looks like a Wobbler with a 1300s style hat and tunic that have stripes. Its bow is different from the regular [https://totally-accurate-battle-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Archer Archer's] bow, being colored light grey with what appears to be pieces of leather wrapped around in a few places, as well as team colored leather around the handle. The arrows also have a team colored ball behind the arrowhead, which disappear on hit and appear to expand into balloons. Balloons are still shown to appear if the arrows hit the environment instead of a unit.
