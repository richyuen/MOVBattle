## Characteristics
The King wields the King's Sword, a towering [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_of_swords#Handedness Great Sword] that can can kill many weak Units and severely injure the stronger ones. Its bulkiness does not impair its attack speed either, with it having an average cooldown period that can be shortened with each successful hit, giving the impression of a frenzied King. While costing a fortune, the cost will be worth it, as it will take quite a lot to defeat this Unit in battle. In addition, their heavy weight makes them resistant against knockback, letting them press on with little difficulties. Despite being large and weighty, they possess a surprisingly high movement speed and can advance against anyone who oppose them.
## Appearance
The King is a large Wobbler that wields a German Zweihänder sword. They wear a golden bejeweled crown, accompanied with a dirty blonde beard, and don a short-sleeved blouse with golden-yellow designs around the collar and sleeves. The King wears an elegant medium-sized cape with golden buttons and straps. They have a thick basic leather brown belt with a golden buckle. They also dawn a pair of puffy pantaloons with golden yellow folds. The King wears leggings with brown, buckled shoes.
