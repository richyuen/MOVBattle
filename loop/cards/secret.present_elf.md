## Characteristics
The Present Elf throws a present at the opponent, which opens after around 3 seconds, releasing a random unit (including un-spawnable sub-units, like Da Vinci or Traveller) as an ally. After throwing the Present, they will proceed by punching the opposing Units.
## Appearance
The Present Elf looks like an average sized Wobbler. The Present Elf dresses like an elf, with a white shirt, a team-colored vest, green pants and shoes. The Present Elf holds a team-color present with yellow accents and some bells as well as a candy cane.
