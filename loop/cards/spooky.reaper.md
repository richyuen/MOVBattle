## Characteristics
The Reaper is an exceptionally powerful offensive unit with three different damaging abilities. Their scythe will burst a dark magic shockwave when swung. Their tentacles will impale any weak Units and hang them as an effigy, and will continuously impale the weakling multiple times if it's a 1v1. However, for the stronger ones, it will use its Scythe to keep its distance, while at the same time doing major damage.
## Appearance
The Reaper closely resembles its origins of the mythological [http://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Death_(personification) Grim Reaper] : A tall skeleton wearing a dark hood and dark tattered robe as well as wielding a scythe and floating above the air. However, it also has 6 dark tendrils from Tokyo Ghoul, used for turning enemy units into pincushions.
