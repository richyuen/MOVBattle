## Characteristics
The Bank Robbers are a duo of outlaws carrying an enormous safe, carrying it nefariously until they come across an enemy unit from 7 away, where they will hurl the safe at the approaching assailants. Upon landing, the safe will burst open and release stacks of cash, as well as creating a shockwave that will injure or kill everyone in its 5 WM radius. The safe will stay on the ground for a while, acting as a barrier for passage before slowly sinking into the ground. After releasing the safe, the Bank Robbers will proceed to attack enemy units with their fists. They are very frail and are easily taken out. If one or both of the Bank Robber gets taken out before the safe is thrown, they will prematurely hurl the safe and the surviving Bank Robber will continue with fisticuffs. Same goes for the safe, which has its own hitpoints.
## Appearance
Bank robbers appears to be two wobblers carrying a safe. The Units have bandanas covering their pores, with a button-up shirt, cowboy hats, cowboy boots, and a leather belt that has two handguns in leather pouches, the taller one has only one handgun in a leather pouch and has an ammo belt on the other side of its hip. When the Vault is being thrown, money is trailing it, and when it hits the ground, the vault explodes open and money comes flying out of it.
