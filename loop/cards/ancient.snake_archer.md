## Characteristics
The Snake Archer shoots snakes from its bow at a fairly high range, which deal damage on impact. After impact these snakes become units and move quickly, lunge at enemies to attack them, and can even be possessed by the player. In addition to being targeted and vulnerable by enemy units, snakes will die after successfully biting twice, or after a 30 second lifetime. Enemies will prefer targeting and advancing toward units other than the snakes, if there are any such units in range, but the snakes will otherwise force a distraction. While a Snake Archer's snakes actively participate in battle, they do not count towards their team's population and their team will lose if only snakes remain, similar to the Scarecrow's crows or the CLAM's clams.
## Appearance
The Snake Archer appears as a Wobbler that is either red or blue (Depends on the team it is on) wearing a Chalcidian helmet and garb. It wields a bow decorated to look like a snake that is also loaded with a snake. It has a quiver full of arrows despite not using them.
