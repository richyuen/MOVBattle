## Characteristics
The Dragon is a Chinese dragon on poles that can breath fire. The Dragon will automatically burn enemy units once in range, units that caught on fire will quickly burn to death and spread fire to nearby units. Friendly units may be burnt by the Dragon too. It is held by four Dragon Carriers that will kick enemy units that gets close, and will drop the dragon and begin punching and kicking once the Dragon itself is dead.
## Appearance
The Dragon is a colored paper Chinese dragon on poles that has a flamethrower installed in its mouth. It is held by four Dragon Carriers with Chinese hair and clothes, also with sandals.
