## Characteristics
The Scarecrow is a fragile but immensely powerful unit armed with a Crow in both hands. They will release them from a medium range. While they have moderately low health yet big size with only average movement speed, they are really expensive due to the immense speed and power of their Crows and their seemingly unlimited supply of them. In combat, the Scarecrow releases their Crows at a fast speed but have an extremely long reload time, keeping them from becoming too overpowered and unstoppable. They will go into an A-pose when reloading, before going back to the T-pose when reloaded, waiting for their crows to perch onto their arms. Even though only holding one in their hand, they release their murder of crows in batches of three, adding up to six with each full attack. They also have a pair of Big Crow Wings, keeping them hovered and negating any fall damage.
## Appearance
The Scarecrow is a Wobbler of big size holding a Crow in both of their hands. They wear a mask that covers their whole head, with team-colored buttons for eyes. They wear a team-colored long sleeves shirt that has a patch on it, along with hay sticking out of their cuffs. They also wear a pair of brown pants, with two patches, and are suspended with two straps on either side by a team-colored button; their ragged clothing is patched with team-colored cloths stitched together with twine. Furthermore, they sport a pair of brown shoes tightened with rope and hay sticking out. At their back is a pair of big, majestic, and mighty black wings; each of their wings has a team-colored wrist. One Crow rests on each elbow as well; unlike their unit counterparts, these Crows do not have wings.
