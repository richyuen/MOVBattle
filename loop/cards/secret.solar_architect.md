## Characteristics
The Solar Architect wields two sun wands that can shoots rays of light at enemy units. When close to a unit, they will cast a golden spinning maelstrom that blasts enemy units away from an area and heals all ally units within it, before imploding and sucking any nearby enemy units in.
## Appearance
The Solar Architect uses the Wobbler unit base. The Solar Architect is bald, but has a giant Decorative Halo over its head, and wears white garments, with golden outlines. They also have some team-colored, ankle-length pants, also outlined in gold, and has white shoes to finish it all off, and for the units weapons, it holds two rods, each with a golden ring on the main end.
