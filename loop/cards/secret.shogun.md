## Characteristics
The Shogun (Formerly known as the Samurai Master) is a melee unit that once was belonging to the Dynasty Faction but now belongs to the Secret Faction. The Shogun is an upgraded, stronger version of the Samurai with more abilities. (Open Alpha) - The Samurai Master will charge at the nearest enemy Unit, when they get in close range they will jump/front flip over the enemy and will usually hit the head of their enemy with their sword while in mid-air, similar to an Axe. (Closed Alpha) - The Samurai Master will charge at the nearest enemy unit and when in close range, instead of jumping over their target and hitting them in mid-air, they swing their sword downwards much like Axes and Barbarians. (Early Access) - The Shogun will walk towards enemy units and when in range, dash towards them and attack with its Reverse Jintachi.
## Appearance
The Shogun appears as a Wobbler either red or blue. The Shogun also holds a sort of reverse-handled Katana with both hands. On its head, the shogun wears a kabuto helmet, a Sashimono (samurai flag) on its back, and wears a detailed menpō mask. It wears Japanese lamellar armor, which is a kind of light armor. In the Closed Alpha, they used to be called the Samurai Master, and the tip of their katana used to glow.
