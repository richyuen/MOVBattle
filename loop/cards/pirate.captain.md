## Characteristics
The Captain is the mini boss of the Pirate Faction. They are big and tough, wielding a Cutlass and their powerful Dragon Pistol. In battle, the Captain will charge in, blasting enemy units with their Pistol, and will hack slash their way through enemy lines. As a more reactive sword wielded, they can parry with their Cutlass, and will additionally kick enemy units in melee combat.
## Appearance
The Pirate Captain wears a long, team-colored coat with a shirt under it and a cloth around the shirt. They also have beige pants, black boots, a long, brown beard with a moustache, a black hat with a painted skull and crossbones, and two parrots. It uses a Cutlass and a Dragon Pistol.
