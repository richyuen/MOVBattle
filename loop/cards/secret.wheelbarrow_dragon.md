## Characteristics
The Wheelbarrow Dragon is a wheelbarrow with a little mechanical dragon attached that breathes fire.
## Appearance
The Wheelbarrow Dragon looks like the original Wheelbarrow unit, except inside the wheelbarrow, the Farmer and Apple Thrower are replaced with a miniature dragon that spews out fire from its mouth. However, it still retains that goofy Traveller Unit, with their Afro and overalls.
