## Characteristics
The Skeleton Archer is the primary ranged Unit of the Spooky Faction. It wields a bow with flaming arrows. Unlike conventional arrows, the fire arrows will burst those who got shot into flames. The flames will spread to nearby units as well.
## Appearance
The Skeleton Archer is a Stiffy that wears a rugged Archer hood with a tattered cape, and a belt. It wields a bow made out of bones, and shoots flaming arrows.
