## Characteristics
The Spear Thrower is a fragile but powerful unit armed with a Throwing Spear. They will use it as a vigorous projectile at enemy units from a short range. As with most ranged units, they have low health and average speed, but very cheap for a ranged unit. In combat, the Spear Thrower throws their primitive javelin at a fast speed and has an average reload time; their spear flies at a slow speed and deals high damage. They will go put their hands down when reloading, before raising another fresh spear to hurl at their opponents.
## Appearance
The Spear Thrower is a Wobbler of average size holding a Throwing Spear. They have a pair of animal tusks wrapped with straps and ropes on the side of their head like a headband, while wearing a brown loincloth tied with a rope that is ornamented with bones and a skull to cover their groin. They also wear a necklace made from a beast's fang tied with a strap and ropes.
