## Characteristics
The Hoplite is a combination of the Sarissa and the Shield Bearer, and is a well balanced unit of the Ancient Faction. They are equipped with a spear and shield, giving them some protection and decent offensive options. Compared to the prementioned units though, the Hoplite's shield is much smaller, and their spear is half the size in comparison. Still, they work well in formations, and can overwhelm a big unit or ranged squads easily.
## Appearance
The Hoplite appears as a Wobbler wearing either red or blue muscle cuirass while holding an aspis (An ancient rounded shield used by the Greeks). In their left or right hands, they hold a dory (A spear used by Hoplites). They used to be all blue/red colored depending on the team.
