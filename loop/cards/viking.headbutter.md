## Characteristics
The Headbutter is a cheap melee unit belonging to the Viking Faction. They attack by ramming the opponent with their spiked helmets in the form of a headbutt, thought the helmet does not actually increase the damage. They have good health and damage for their price but have a long attack cooldown. In addition, they can be seen flipping over enemy units from time to time.
## Appearance
The Headbutter Unit wears a helmet with one single spike. They also possess long, braided hair, as well as thick furry boots. To attack, they ram into foes by head butting them. After the initial attack, they get up and attack by slapping their opponents, until the next headbutt is ready.
