## Characteristics
The Gunslinger is one of the strongest Units in the Wild West faction. It wields two revolvers in each hand and shoots bullets upwards, which fall back down onto units, as if it's raining bullets.
## Appearance
The Gunslinger has a dark, bowler-like team color hat and black sideburns. It wears a beige undershirt and a buttoned over-shirt with its team's color, with a purple tie which seems very wide. The gunslinger also has a jacket over their shirt that is team colored with a purple handkerchief. It also wears pants with it's team's color, black shoes, and two empty gun holsters. It wields two Bullet Rain Revolvers in each hand.
