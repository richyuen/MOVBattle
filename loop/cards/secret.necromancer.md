## Characteristics
The Necromancer will throw a skull projectile from their ghoulish lantern, and if a unit is hit, they will slowly perish, rotting away until their ultimate demise, in which they will be resurrected as a Skeleton of the Necromancer's team. They also can summon 3 graves from the ground, pushing anyone away if they're starting to close the distance. Finally, it is equipped with a sickle in its right hand that inflicts heavy damage.
## Appearance
The Necromancer appears to be a generic Stiffy, wearing a leather cap with 2 horns protruding on each side. Their clothes are tattered and is made out of two crudely stitched together pieces of cloth while its pants are mere shorts, and his only foot protection are some worn out boots. They also have two metallic armbands on each Humerus. It appears to have a magical, team-colored kerosene lantern, with a ghostly flame inside, lit by a tiny wax candle. The blue ghost-flame also is seen encircling the lantern, in a counter-clockwise manner at rather fast speeds. The Necromancer also wields a Sickle, though it is considerably scraped down, with multiple tiny bits of the blade missing. Interestingly, the handle of the sickle doesn't seem to be made out of the same material as the hook, instead being made out of bone.
