## Characteristics
The Executioner is a towering figure with a disturbing appearance. This hooded unit uses their monstrous bearded axe to jump and chop any enemies they encounter. The Executioner is larger than the average Wobbler and possesses a large health-pool.
## Appearance
The Executioner wears a black cloth over their head and face. They wear trousers and a grey metal vest. It wields an axe that is much taller than itself and holds it similar to the Halberd from the Renaissance Faction.
