## Characteristics
The Ballooner is a kamikaze unit that will use their Ballooner Grabs to glue onto units and lift them up into the sky with their Balloon Backpack. Glued units without attack abilities like a kick will be unable to counter-attack and will float as long as they are lightweight, until the balloon detaches from the backpack where both units ignite to the ground. Unlike their victim, the Ballooner is immune to fall damage and may turn into a giant fireball when descending. They will continue to attack after landing, but will be unable to lift units again as their Balloon Backpack is one-use only.
## Appearance
The Ballooner wears leather garments and goggles, carrying a backpack with a mini hot air balloon attached to it. It also has arms covered with "glue" to catch enemies. Before that, they used to have different colored pant-legs, rather than its pantaloons.
