## Characteristics
The Catapult is a crowd control artillery of the medieval faction. It attacks by hurling boulders at opponents. These cannot be blocked by any Projectile Shields and tend to crush lines of enemy Units. When out of range, the catapult will be pushed until it reaches its range. The Catapult breaks into pieces once destroyed, whether by melee or ranged.
## Appearance
The Catapult is quite large and has a Wobbler in a simple coif cap along with a shirt and a pair of belt-held pants and shoes holding onto the bucket, which must pull back the boulder before firing. The Catapult can be found on multiple campaign levels in the Open/Closed Alpha. The rocks that the Catapult fires have a mini-explosion dealing tons of AOE damage. It has banners with a crown of the color that the Catapult is on the side. In the Wild West update, Landfall jokingly gave the Catapult eyes. It was reverted in the following update.
