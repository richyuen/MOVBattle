## Characteristics
The Archer is a ranged Unit enlisted within the Medieval Faction. They have long range, reasonable accuracy, and sufficient damage per hit. They are one of the most basic ranged Units in the game. In the full game, their cheaper counterpart are the Poachers, whom have shorter range.
## Appearance
The Archer is a Wobbler wearing a hood with a liripipe along with a medieval shirt and pants. They also wear vambraces and have a pair of archer boots.
