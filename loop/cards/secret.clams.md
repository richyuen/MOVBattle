## Characteristics
CLAMS is a unit summoner, being able to throw dozens of weak but quick Clams at enemy units. They are frail though, and need to get in range before they can attack. Combined with the cheerleader unit it is a very powerful unit
## Appearance
It wears a rolled-up shirt and a rope around its pants that acts as a belt, as well as pants and shoes with ropes around them. Its hands are clams with eyes still on them and it has a clam hat.
