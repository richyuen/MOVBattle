## Characteristics
The Potionseller is a fragile and weak unit armed with a Potion in both hands. They will throw them at enemy units from a medium range. Even though low in health and only having average movement speed, they are somewhat expensive due to the unique drunk properties of their strong brew. In combat, the Potionseller throws their Potions at a very fast speed and have a relatively quick reload; their Potions fly at a relatively slow speed and deals very low damage. However, upon impact, the Potion will splash and effect anyone in the splash zone with drunkenness, making them green and confused; they may even splash themselves when thrown up-close. These tipsy units will fail to concentrate on attacking and wander about; they may even struggle to stand and walk. Sadly, this effect does not deal damage overtime, and will wear off after a few seconds depending on the dose.
## Appearance
The Potionseller is a Wobbler of average size holding a Potion in both of their hands. They have an unusually round head with umber brown hair. They wear a dark team-colored jerkin with a light team-colored bottom tighten with four rope. Each side has a copper button above the chest of the vest. They also wear a brown undershirt underneath the vest and a pair of dark team-colored hosen along with a pair of brown boots.
