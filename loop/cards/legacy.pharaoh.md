## Characteristics
The Pharaoh is a unit from Legacy Faction who forces enemy units to stop before it and kneel, similar to the Banner Bearer, but as a weapon against the enemy.
## Appearance
The Pharaoh wears a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modius_(headdress) Modius] Crown with golden bands, garb and wields the Crook and Flail, which are symbols of the pharaoh in Egyptian times.
