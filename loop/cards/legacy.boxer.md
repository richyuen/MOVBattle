## Characteristics
The Boxer is a melee Unit belonging to the Legacy Faction. This Unit punches the enemy Units rapidly. It used to be in the Miscellaneous Faction during the Alpha stage of development but it is now in the Legacy Faction.
## Appearance
The Boxer wears boxing headgear, Gloves, and shorts. It also wears shin guards along with shoes and a bandage on the unit's left cheek. The boxing gloves it wields are team colored, with a white part showing the palm of the glove.
