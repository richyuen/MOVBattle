## Characteristics
The Infernal Whip wields two flaming whips that will extend toward enemy units, lighting them on fire as well as inflicting considerable damage to anyone close. However, once the enemy closed in, the Infernal Whip is as good as dead, as while they may be somewhat endurant, it can't hold a prolonged fight, so consider keeping them in the backlines.
## Appearance
The Infernal Whip appears to be a generic Wobbler. However, this one burns... literally. It has solid igneous rock on its head, and wears a normal shirt (team-colored) with darker but still team-colored sleeves, with seeming magma armbands. The belt also appears to be made of magma, along with its kneecap armor, whereas for the rest of the lower half, is merely team-colored pants and boots.
