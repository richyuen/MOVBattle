## Characteristics
The Ice Archer is the main ranged unit of the Viking faction. They are equipped with an Ice Bow and Arrows. Their Ice Arrows deals no damage, but rather, it will freeze enemy units, causing them to move slower and freeze all together when they are dead. High health units have a higher tolerance to the effects.
## Appearance
The Ice Archer is a Wobbler in Viking clothes and helmet. The Ice Archer and the bow are covered in snow and ice and will shiver while standing.
