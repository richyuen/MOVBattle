## Characteristics
The Jester is a Unit with proficiency in dodging. They make use of their nimbleness to the fullest by front-flipping out of harm's way. They are fitted with sharp daggers on their hands and toes, which they utilize by stabbing hand-to-hand and delivering a lethal kick respectively. As proficient in killing and dodging the Jester is, they are incredibly fragile and can easily succumb to minor injuries. They are also unable to dodge Projectiles from ranged weapons.
## Appearance
The Jester is clad in a team-colored jester outfit and hat. They have two knives strapped to its sleeves and two knives on its shoes.
