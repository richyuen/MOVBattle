## Characteristics
The Flag Bearer is a Legacy Faction unit that wields a large flag. The flag represents its own team color on the fabric, and it also boosts its teammates' movement speed. It also makes a weird and long sound.
## Appearance
The Flag Bearer wields a flag with its own team color on the fabric. military uniform It holds the flag up just like the original scrapped unit, the Napoleonic Flag Bearer. Before the battle, a colored circle is shown around the Flag Bearer, helping distinguish which units are being buffed. This is the same as the Banner Bearer and other banner bearing units.
