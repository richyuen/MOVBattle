## Characteristics
The Tree Giant is a humongous vagrant who uses a green, lush tree to smack those who are enemies with them.
## Appearance
The Tree Giant looks like a gigantic Wobbler with brown hair and a tied beard, a green shirt with two brown wrappings at the waist, brown ragged shorts, and brown sandals on their feet. They actually have no clothes unique to their appearance, as the props listed belong to the Berserker, Farmer (albeit recolored), Bomb Thrower, and Artemis respectively.
