## Characteristics
The Bomb Thrower excels in crowd control, they are equipped with bombs in both hands in which they will throw. The bombs create a big explosion, causing heavy area damage to units.
## Appearance
The Bomb Thrower wields two bombs, and wears a vest, belt, bomb pouch, ripped shorts, bandana, striped socks, and shoes.
