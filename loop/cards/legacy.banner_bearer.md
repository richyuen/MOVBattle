## Characteristics
The Banner Bearer is a Legacy faction unit who wields a large banner that it uses to keep ally Units stationary.
## Appearance
The Unit appears to be wearing an early 19th-century "shako" cap and military uniform. They carry a banner pole with a golden pommel and fittings. At the top of the staff is a cross-beam, and two small banners hang from the top of the cross-beam.
