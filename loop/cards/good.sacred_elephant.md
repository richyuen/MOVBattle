## Characteristics
The Sacred Elephant carries a seriously powerful Elephant Rider that throws two deadly spears at enemy units. Each spear, when thrown, will deal high damage as well as sucking nearby units and doing minor damage. Akin to the much well known Mammoth, the elephant will charge and stomp enemy units, albeit much weaker than their ancestors. The elephant and the Spear Thrower are not bound by health and can be separated even if none of them were killed.
## Appearance
The Elephant seems to be a mixture of an [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_elephant African Elephant] and an [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_elephant Asian Elephant], with a golden ring for each tusk. It wears a golden headpiece, and has a strap around its neck in which is connected to the grand saddle/seat, also made of gold, but also with team-colored cushions. However, where the saddle should be is actually covered by a blanket, also team-colored. The Rider itself appears to have some sort of tied hat, with an ovular crystal, like gold, dead center. Its hair is completely white. It wears a white short tank-top, with gold linings here and there. It also wears baggy white shorts and sandals.
