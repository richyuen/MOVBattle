## Characteristics
The Super Peasant is the boss unit in the Legacy Faction. They fly at extremely high speeds and will attack every unit. The Super Peasant is also extremely durable.
## Appearance
The Super Peasant looks like the Peasant, but it has spiked, blonde hair, and also has two glowing bracelets on both wrists, along with glowing eyes, similar to the Super Boxer. It has a shirt nearly identical to the normal peasant, but white, and lacking the long-sleeved undershirt. For the bottomwear, it wears white, rolled up pants, but lacks the socks and shoes that it once had.
