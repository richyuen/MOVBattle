## Characteristics
The Farmer is a fragile unit armed with a Pitchfork. They will use it to swing and stab enemy units. Low health and slightly slow movement speed, they are the poor person's infantry with their extremely low cost. When engaging, the Farmer will swiftly step in the enemy unit at close range, followed by a swing from their Pitchfork. In combat, their Pitchfork deals very low damage with a slow cooldown. They will also slowly back up while attacking, before swinging ahead with a much better stab. One would also find the Farmer as one of the passengers on the Wheelbarrow, stabbing any enemy units at close-quarters as well as having more endurance.
## Appearance
The Farmer is a Wobbler of average size wielding a Pitchfork. They wear a team-colored hood with a long liripipe at the end of it, along with a team-colored shirt with its bottom cut into a leaf-like pattern. Furthermore, they wear a pair of team-colored knee-length shorts belted with straps. In addition, they wear a pair of long white socks along with a pair of brown medieval slip-on shoes.
