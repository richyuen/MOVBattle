## Characteristics
The Skeleton Giant is a ginormous Skeleton Warrior. They will send everyone who dares attack them in horrible fright. In addition, they can kick units who dare to stand against them.
## Appearance
The Skeleton Giant, unsurprisingly, is a Stiffy. It is dressed in ragged shorts, a Greek-style helmet and greaves. It wields a "small" shield and a sword. When it is killed, it collapses into bones. It makes rattling noises, and occasionally growls or roars in a wheezy, hollow voice. The unit is also very big compared to other units.
