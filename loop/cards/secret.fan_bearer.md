## Characteristics
The Fan Bearer uses their long handheld fan in an attempt to blow away approaching enemies. They are very fragile and exposed. The wind they create can cause extremely light damage; if close enough, they will smack enemies with their fan and cause minor damage. A lone Fan Bearer is often insufficient to keep a normal Wobbler at bay, with two being required to hold them off consistently. The wind blows every unit in its area, bunching up will provide no resistance at all.
## Appearance
The Fan Bearer is a Wobbler wearing ancient egyptian clothes, with a sash over its right shoulder and a three piece skirt, all of which are different shades of team color. They wear wrist guards and shoes identical to those of the Sarissa, and wield a long poled, large team colored fan in the same manner of the unit.
