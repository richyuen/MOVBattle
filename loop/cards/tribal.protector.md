## Characteristics
The Protector is a weak unit armed with a Wood Shield. They use it to bash and batter enemy units while shielding them from certain incoming projectiles. While they have low health and average speed, their shields let them endure more attacks by blocking projectiles and feeble melee attacks In combat, The Protector's Wood Shield deals very low damage (25) but has a short cooldown period; they bash violently and are quite aggressive with the shield. However, the Protector is vulnerable from behind, and their shield has a round opening that exposes their face to incoming projectiles, (note that protectors can’t always block projectiles like spears, fire arrows, and ice arrows).
## Appearance
The Protector is a Wobbler of average size holding a Wood Shield. They wear a skull as a helmet that covers the top half of their head, along with a brown garment held by a shoulder strap on one side. They also wear a necklace made out of three skulls from small critters with a strap tied through their orbits.
