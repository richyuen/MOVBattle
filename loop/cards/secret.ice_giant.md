## Characteristics
The Ice Giant is a colossal titan with terrifically long arms. While lumber and clumsy, its long arms are very powerful, able to create shockwaves of ice with each punch that connects with a surface. They can also blow a cold breeze, freezing units. Along with its high health, the Ice Giant is the largest unit in the game and the "boss" of the Secret faction.
## Appearance
The Nordic Ice Giant looks like a huge humanoid, ape-like creature, distinct from giant Wobblers, covered in snow on its head, shoulders, arms, and torso. It has a very muscular build with a small waist and giant long arms relative to size. As it is a Viking faction secret unit, it wears a belt that has a couple of Brawler shields hanging on the sides. It has an icicle symbol on its belt rather than a Landfall logo, which may be to signify its greater power than the other Viking units.
