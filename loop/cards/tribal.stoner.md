## Characteristics
The Stoner is a fragile but powerful unit armed with a huge Boulder in their hands. Despite their skinny stature, they can (and will) toss their rock at enemy units with tremendous power from a close range. Stat-wise, they have low health, slightly slow speed, and a cheap price. In combat, the Stoner tosses their boulder at a fast speed and have a slightly long reload time; their stone flies at a slow speed and deals high damage. The immense power that comes with the tossed stone can even crush shielded units; though the knockback power of the stone diminishes as the unit gets bigger. Before thrown, their stone can also somewhat protect the Stoner by blocking certain projectiles that are coming at their torso.
## Appearance
The Stoner is a Wobbler of average size carrying a Stone. They have long, messy brown hair reminiscent of that of the Clubber but without the beard and with a darker shade of brown, and wears a simple brown garment held by a shoulder strap on their right side. They also wear a necklace made out of three bones tied to a strap with twine.
