## Characteristics
The Hay Baler is a fragile and weak but greatly shielded unit armed with a Stick. They will use it to swing, beat and whack enemy units. Poor and low health with only average speed, they resorted to wearing haybales as armor, and totally accurately, the haybales are more protective than real armor, being able to absorb most types of projectiles, like fire, and even ballista bolts. Such budget cuts makes them a very cheap unit. In combat, the Hay Baler's Stick deals low damage but have a very fast cooldown. Even though very effective against projectiles, their haybale cannot protect against melee attacks; their arms, legs and head are also exposed, leaving them vulnerable to getting shot.
## Appearance
The Hay Baler is a Wobbler of average size wielding a Stick. They have a brown bowl cut hair and wears a thick haybale. The haybale has blades of hay sticking out at its outer edges and is tied with two straps around the center. They also wear a pair of brown simple shoes that are tightened with rope.
