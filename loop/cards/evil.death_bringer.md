## Characteristics
The Death Bringer brings death to all by charging at enemy units and cutting through all of them with their two red hot tonfa blades in a swift fashion. It also has the ability to dodge projectiles, moving to the side then back in almost an instant.
## Appearance
The Death Bringer carries two blades, has a demon skull on its head, lots of feathers, a belt with bones, pants, and cloth wrapping around its legs and waist.
