## Characteristics
The Halfling is a moderately fragile and very weak unit armed with naught but their Halfling Grab hands. They will try their best to punch any enemy units they encounter. With moderately low health and small size with only average speed, their underwhelming stats are made-up with their extremely cheap price. When engaging, the Halfling will jump into enemy unit when up-close. Even though vulnerable after landing, the Halfling's tiny proportions makes them hard to hit overall. In combat, their tiny hand deals extremely low damage with a long cooldown. They will frequently grab on to enemy units, trapping them; they will even do so after said unit is dead, taking the poor corpse along the journey.
## Appearance
The Halfling is a humanoid that is half the size of the average Wobbler. They have a bushy brown hair, distinctively hairy feet and wears three layers of clothing; a team-colored jacket, a light team-colored vest and a white shirt underneath. In addition, they wear a pair of dark team-colored shorts buckled with a buckle belt.
