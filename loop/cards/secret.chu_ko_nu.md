## Characteristics
The Chu Ko Nu's crossbow is unique compared to other ranged weapons, as it fires 5 bolts consecutively in one attack. The unit has a slow reload time in-between firing sprees, but is strongest in groups. Unlike other ranged units, they have a melee AI, meaning they will keep backing off until they reach their weapon's maximum range from their enemies.
## Appearance
They appear as a Chinese soldier with their hair in a bun like other Dynasty-themed units. They have armor and a unique quiver, as well as a shirt, belt, pants, and shoes. They are equipped with a repeating crossbow, a weapon invented in ancient China.
