## Characteristics
The Devout Gauntlet wields two glowing bladed gauntlets as well as an intimidating mustache, using them to charge and punch enemy units. Being the ethereal fighters they are, these warriors are the main troop that you can find in the Fantasy Good Faction.
## Appearance
The Devout Gauntlet has a golden circlet with a triangular shaped extension at the front with a glowing gem in the center, donning a yellow moustache for facial hair. It wears a yellow band that sprouts into shoulder armor that curves upwards as it goes further out, otherwise lacking any form of torso accessories. Gray arm wraps over two thirds of its arms, ending off at the hands with golden fists with individual fingers, shaped to properly wield their respective weapon. Missing shoes, it instead has long large and baggy beige pants that extend outwards to a great amount by the time they reach the feet of the unit, a team colored trim ending them off. Around its waist is a team colored band that keeps the pants secure, the top of said pants branching upwards and outwards over it, showing how incredibly big the clothing is.
