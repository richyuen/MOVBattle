## Characteristics
The Raptor is a bipedal animal with deadly teeth and claws. Agile and deadly, these speedy predators can easily run up to opposing units and maul them vigorously. Unfortunate units who got under the dinosaur will also get painfully trampled by their powerful legs. The Raptor's main method of attack is their deadly bite, a swift snap on the enemy that can at times attach them to the mouth of sharp teeth that the Raptor has, allowing them to constantly attack instead of doing a bite and run. The bite has a short cooldown period after and attack. Trampling on the other hand will cause damage as long as the Raptor is stomping the unit with their hind legs; simply ramming will no cause any damage. Occasionally, the Raptor may be excited and hop around. While it looks derpy, their bounciness makes them unpredictable to hit even when they are not running.
## Appearance
It is a raptor, with what appear to be feathers and a tongue corresponding to its team's colors. It always looks like its lying down when it attacks a unit. The end of its tail which tapers down has feathers on either side of it. It also has spots near its spine and a tongue color corresponding to the color of its team. It also has greenish-brownish skin when looking from certain angles.
