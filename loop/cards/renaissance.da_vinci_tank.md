## Characteristics
The Da Vinci Tank is a deadly artillery unit and the most expensive unit from the Renaissance faction. The tank itself is circular and has sixteen cannons attached to it. During battle, the machine spins around, launching cannonballs from the cannons. The cannonballs deal moderate damage on impact, and shatter into fragments. The Da Vinci Tank is almost completely invulnerable to small melee units like Squires or Clubbers, because the cannons themselves actually deal damage while the tank is spinning and their weapons are too short to hit the tank itself.
## Appearance
The Da Vinci Tank is a mobile hut-shaped tank with 16 cannons facing around the tank in a circular pattern. The tank will spin around and begin firing the cannons at units if in range. The tank has colorful wooden panels around the dome of the vehicle that act as armor. Inside the vehicle is the mastermind, controlling the entire device: Da Vinci.
