## Characteristics
The Healer (formerly known as the Priest) is the first healing unit in TABS. When healing, the Healer releases a gold beam onto the nearest and most endurant unit. They are pacifists, and won't attack, and they will remain still if there are no teammates. With no armor or dodging skills, they are very vulnerable, be sure to have a unit that can protect them.
## Appearance
The Healer appears as an old Wobbler holding a staff with a Globus Cruciger on the top and wearing a priest robe decorated with two crossed bands and having the tonsure. When the Healer heals, a golden beam connects to the unit the healer is curing, turning them gold for a moment, then they will go back to their original color.
