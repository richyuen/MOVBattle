## Characteristics
The Shadow Walker skims through darkness and teleports to the farthest enemy in an attempt to backstab them before they can react. Every time a unit is attacked by a Shadow Walker, they emit green particles, with one more for every attack, the more particles, the more damage they take from ALL Shadow Walkers, meaning using many Shadow Walkers to attack one unit is extremely effective.
## Appearance
The Shadow Walker wears tattered clothing along with spiked boots, bracers, knee-pads and helmet. They use a bone-like dagger as a weapon.
