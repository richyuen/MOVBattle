## Characteristics
The Exiled Sentinel wields two protective shields that blocks most incoming projectiles, which will turn into deadly axes when within melee range.
## Appearance
The Exiled Sentinel uses the generic Wobbler base. They have ginger hair, a Norman-Style Pavise shield per hand, and a spiked uniform, ranging from its hat to its bracers and leggings; the three of which are all covered in spikes. When in close range, their shields will turn into two bloodied Horseman’s Axes.
