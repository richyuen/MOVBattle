## Characteristics
The Radiant Glaive is equipped with a shield that protects them from most incoming projectiles as well as a throwing glaive. The glaive inflicts low damage and its projectile can bounces between units, acting as a less powerful version of Zeus. The number it can bounce depends on the enemy's unit weight; the higher it is, the less it will bounce.
## Appearance
The Radiant Glaive has a golden square shaped helmet decorated with a team colored strand that pokes out the back, gold and team colored diamond shapes, the latter over the former, placed at the front. It has a team colored vest trimmed with gold, a baggy white undershirt with uneven sleeves, golden cuffs to go with them, and a cape of the same color draped over the back of its vest with a singular gold line running along the bottom of it. Over its legs it wears team colored pants with golden colors scattered on it, a half diamond shape made at the start of them and two lines of the same color trimmed at the knees and a third down the shins. Lastly, it dons short white shoes with a singular golden band each that have a diamond attachment on both sides.
