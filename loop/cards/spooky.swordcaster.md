## Characteristics
The Swordcaster can be quite devastating, especially in groups. It is, however, susceptible to being killed by ranged attacks, as the range of its swords are not as far as most ranged units, and its health, while above average, is still low, making it better if protected by other units.
## Appearance
The unit has 8 blue longswords slowly orbiting around it. It has a bowl haircut and fantasy prison clothes. Its hands are bound in a lock and appears to be wearing a locked, heavy cuirass. It hovers slightly above the ground and floats around during battle.
