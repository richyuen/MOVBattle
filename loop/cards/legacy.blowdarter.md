## Characteristics
The Blowdarter is a ranged Unit that once belonged to the Aztec Faction, but now belongs to the Legacy Faction. The Unit attacks with a blowdart gun, which appears to do little to no damage to an enemy unit with every dart shot, but when a dart hits a target, the hit unit gets poisoned and slowly dies.
## Appearance
The Blowdarter appears as a Wobbler either red or blue while holding a blowgun placed against its lower pores. It wears a feathery scarf-type accessory and brown pants with a team-color band. They also have feathery knee guards.
