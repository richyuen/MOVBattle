## Characteristics
The Jouster is a dangerous cavalry unit of the Renaissance faction. The Jouster controls its horse's direction while mounted atop it, and can trample smaller units under hoof. When attacking, the lance rapidly does damage to enemies it's in contact with, being able to kill tanky units like Monks with one good thrust. It also has a shield that can block projectiles/weapons and push enemies out of the way. The rider themself, though, is quite fragile. While it is able to walk and fight if the horse is killed, the steed dies if its rider dies atop it. The Jouster Horse has the ability to create shockwaves wherever they canter. These shockwaves emanate from around their torso and damages enemy units who are within their 2 radius, effectively trampling those who came into their path. Naturally, they have to be speeding in order to inflict damage.
## Appearance
The rider has Renaissance-style armor and is wielding a lance and shield. The lance has spirals of the lancer's team color and white. Both the rider and the horse have colored feathers depending on the team's color. The horse that it rides has a cloth wrapping around its body which the color corresponds with the team.
