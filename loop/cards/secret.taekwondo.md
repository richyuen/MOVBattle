## Characteristics
The Taekwondo attack by delivering a kick to enemy units they encounter, often flinging them away. Serving as their main method of attack, their kicks are the one of most powerful strikes of any units in-game. Their kicks are also the quickest to perform with the shortest cooldown period. While waiting for the Taekwondo Kick's cooldown, the Taekwondo will punch their enemies instead. Unlike their deadly footwork however, their prowess with fisticuffs are much weaker. Although dangerous in melee, the Taekwondo lacks defense from attacks and their hitpoints are slightly above average at best.
