## Characteristics
The Pirate Queen is a melee specialist and main Boss of the Pirate Faction that duel wields a Cutlass and Sabre. The Pirate Queen can parry incoming attacks with their tow cutlasses and is a rather deadly striker. The Pirate Queen will also kick enemy units. If outnumbered, the Pirate Queen will spin and release several bombs, then proceed to helicopter upwards, with the bombs blowing up the units below it.
## Appearance
The Pirate Queen wears a cloth covering one of their eyes. The Pirate Queen also curly long hair with hair beads as well as long red brown boots, pink pants, a red skirt with green beads around it, a red short-sleeved shirt, and a brown bomb sash. The Pirate Queen wields an unusually big Cutlass and a jewel-embedded custom Sabre.
