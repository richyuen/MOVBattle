## Characteristics
The Samurai Giant is a titanic Japanese warrior. They can deflect big projectiles and perform backflips to squash enemy units that comes close, all the while swinging their gigantic katana which does a significant amount of damage. They are one of the four Giants in the game (The others being the Skeleton Giant, Tree Giant, and the Ice Giant).
## Appearance
The Samurai Giant looks like a magnified Samurai. It will use its katana to strike units when they are far away from it, otherwise it will use their "backflip" ability until units are kept at a certain distance from it. Its katana also has a different guard than both Samurai and Shogun, The Helmet uses the Shogun helmet instead of the normal Samurai Helmet.
