## Characteristics
The Pumpkin Catapult is the Spooky faction's artillery. As it's name suggests, it is a variant of the normal Catapult, with bones and bouncy jack o' lanterns as the payload. It is operated by a Pumpkin Catapult Operator.
## Appearance
The Pumpkin Catapult looks similar to the Medieval counterpart, the Catapult, being a large four-wheeled mechanism with a small operator at the back end. The major difference between the two is that the Pumpkin Catapult is predominantly black with bones, and throws Pumpkins instead of boulders. The operator has been replaced by a skeleton and the boulder holder for a rib cage. Team distinction comes in the form of small banners at both sides of the catapult itself, with the skeleton operator also clad in a tattered clothing of the appropriate team color.
