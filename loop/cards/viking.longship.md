## Characteristics
The Longship is a ship made up of 3 types of sub-units, three Oarsmen aboard carried by 2 Longship Carriers. Once near any enemy units, the carriers will throw the Longship on them. Its impact on the ground will create and AoE effect, crushing any beneath. Oarsmen will disembark and attack enemy units with their oars. It is one of the more powerful kamikaze units in the game.
## Appearance
The ship itself looks like a miniature version of regular Viking longships, having Brawler shields on each side. The shield colour depends on its team. It has what looks like 3 Long-Boaters holding oars. * The Longship is not one unit, but six units since there are sub-units holding it up/riding on it and 2 carriers carrying it. The people riding it are called oarmen.
