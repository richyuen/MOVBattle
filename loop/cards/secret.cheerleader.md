## Characteristics
The Cheerleader is a support unit that boosts the attack rate of the target unit. Units that are being buffed will have multi-colored confetti appear around them. The Cheerleader's buff stacks with more cheerleaders nearby; with enough Cheerleaders, the attack rates of nearby units can be increased dramatically, especially more slow-attacking heavy units such as the King or Jarl, or even artillery weapons such as the Catapult or Ballista. However, since Cheerleaders are totally defenseless and can barely attack, they need enough support to stay alive. As a rather expensive support unit, it's important to consider which units to pair them with are the most effective for your budget and whether they'll be able to eliminate the whole army before any cheerleaders die. In the end, they are often too expensive for more than one or two be to used effectively.
## Appearance
The Cheerleader is a Wobbler wearing a disco outfit along with disco-themed shoes and arm braces.
