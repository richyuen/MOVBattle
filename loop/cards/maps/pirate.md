## Overview
The Pirate (Map) is a large circular map for the Pirate Faction that has two pirate ships that are moored on both sides of an island: each with artillery batteries that can keep Cannons stationary. In the center there's a fairly big area of sand to fight on. In the background, you can see a decently-sized mountain covered in grass and trees as well as a waterfall.
