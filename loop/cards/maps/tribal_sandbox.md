## Overview
Tribal Sandbox is a large Tribal themed map featuring an orange grassy plain surrounded by trees and mountains. This map has many nooks and crannies towards the edges, while the center is very open and allows for large-scale armies to fight. This map allows for many interesting army compositions, since enemies can be hidden behind or on top of the mountains. The Raptor Rider and Raptor can be found here. Most tribal-themed campaign battles use Tribal 1 or Tribal 2, which are much smaller.
