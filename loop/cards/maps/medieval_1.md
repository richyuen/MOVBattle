## Overview
Medieval 1 is a Medieval-themed Map. It is a small "island" surrounded by water; on the left is a forest, and on the right are building/castle ruins, with the two sides connected by a bridge. Due to the size of the map, large armies can't be played. However, there are opportunities for trickery by hiding Units on the various ledges and trees around the map.

## Unlock
Beneath the bridge is a Door with the landfall logo, which can be reached down a ramp on the left side of the island, if the player looks at it long enough they will open it then the player can unlock the fantasy good map, campaign, and faction. Due to a bug the faction is unlocked at the start of the game.
## Secret Units
The following Secret units can be found on this map: * Executioner: Look for an axe on a stump, located in the forest. *Tree Giant: Look for a smaller tree on the edge of the forest. *Jester: Look inside a barrel in the ruins.}}
