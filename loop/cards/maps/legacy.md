## Overview
There is a Church of St. Wobbly Horse and a graveyard around it, forest and Mountains are around the glade, there is a ruined building, along with two camps of either red or blue tents, and there is also a river carving through a mountain in the background.
## Appearance
The Legacy map appears to be a remake of the Classic Map, but considerably different. The church appears to be in ruins, and is mossy on a few sides, with a side transept entrance. though it is locked due to the ongoing battles. The roof is now a dark grey, with an opening leading to the church attic, and the steeple has a rather steep spire on top, unlike last time. Surrounding it is a graveyard. On the other side, there are several graves like the left side.
## Legacy Units
Like the Secret faction, nearly all of the Legacy units are unlocked on this map.
