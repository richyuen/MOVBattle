## Overview
Farmer Snow is a variation of Farmer 1, in which the map is covered in snow. The hay field in the upper-right corner is now a grouping of evergreen trees, and there are lights, trees, and snowmen strewed about. The river is also frozen over, meaning that units can no longer be pushed to the edge of the map and that units can now walk over it.

## Location
Farmer Snow is unlocked via Farmer 1. Behind the taller blue building, there is a snow globe on an overhang. Stare at it and the festive map will be unlocked.
