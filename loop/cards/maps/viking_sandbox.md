## Overview
Viking Sandbox is a large Viking themed map that is set on an icy field and surrounded on all sides by a giant ice wall. On the left side is a large, impenetrable gate. This is just like a giant icy arena. Its flat circular shape makes it ideal for big battles (if you don't want units getting stuck) although, if you go too high, you wont be able to see much because of mist.
