## Overview
Ancient 1 is a large rectangular Map for the Ancient faction. The map is set up where there are two main streets linking the left and right sides of the map. The two streets are very narrow, about three or four units wide. The streets are connected on the ends, and in the middle of the map is an archway that lets units trickle in or stand on top of. Most of the buildings have stairs leading to the rooftops, allowing for more units to walk around.
