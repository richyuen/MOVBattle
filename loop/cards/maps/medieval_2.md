## Overview
Medieval 2, formerly called “Castle,” is a map for the Medieval Faction. It is a medieval battlefield extended to a castle. There is a pond next to the castle.
## Appearance
Medieval 2 appears to be a giant floating plain of land, just like many of the set faction maps. However, there are paths going all sorts of directions, the main one leading to the door of a tall, fantastical ruined castle that probably was home to the Blue team’s King. The Castle walls themselves have a layout of semicircles, each one with its own set of huge crenellations, thought lacking in [https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machicolation machicolations].
