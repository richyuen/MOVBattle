## Overview
Farmer 1 is a map for the Farmer Faction. This map has trees that are similar to Scotland, which are very different from the Tribal maps. The middle of this map has a river going down from the back of the map towards and under a bridge in the middle and dropping off into the void at the front of the map, spinning a watermill that is attached to a large farm house that is separated on both sides of the river.
