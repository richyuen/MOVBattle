## Overview
The Wild West Map is the map for the Wild West Faction. It appears to be a stereotypical Western town with a saloon, hardware shop, and other miscellaneous buildings. It also has four slim areas for units to battle on along with alleyways between buildings. Many cacti surround the edges of the Map.
