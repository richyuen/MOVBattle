## Overview
The Bridge is a Map in TABS, listed as the second Simulation Map option after Simulation (Map) and Bridge With Rails. It is a very minimalist map option, providing two large blocks for each team to place their units, connected by a long and narrow "bridge" that gives the map its name. In the Simulation Campaign, it is used as the first map for Bridge Battle.
## Layout
Like most other Simulation-style maps, the layout of the whole map is stripped down to its most basic geometric shapes, preventing any significant exploitation of props or design quirks to sway the battle in one team's favor. The bridge itself is a long stretch of track that spans 53 unit footprints in length, while also only having around 5 of width.
