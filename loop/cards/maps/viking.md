## Overview
Viking is the Campaign map for the Viking (Faction). It is a large map with a giant frozen lake in the middle used as a battlefield. The map also has snowy trees covering the sides, and to the left, there is a pirate ship that is frozen in ice, along with a frozen waterfall around the top area of the map.
