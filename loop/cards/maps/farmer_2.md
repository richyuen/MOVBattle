## Overview
Farmer 2 is a Map for the Farmer Faction. This Map is separated into eight sections due to the cobble walling and has bushes and trees as well as a large open pathway on the left side, with a single farm house present in the back of the left side. The left side also has half of an open field filled with hay bales in the front middle shared with the right side. To the left of the open hay bale area is a narrow alleyway with a broken fence cutting it off from the open pathway.
