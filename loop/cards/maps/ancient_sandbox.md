## Overview
Ancient Sandbox is a very large Ancient themed island Map with a wide-open field and several small buildings around the edges. To the north on the centerline is a spiral mountain, and there are a few smaller islands off in the distance, but these tend to be too far away to be of any use for units, especially since many will just walk off and fall to their death/drown in the water.
