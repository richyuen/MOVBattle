## Overview
Tribal 2 is a map for the Tribal Faction. This map shares some qualities that Tribal 1 holds. This being a large open field and surrounded by short hills and yellow and orange trees. Unlike Tribal 1 however, is that the right side of the map has placed a mountain with a cave in the bottom middle of it where the campfire was. The mountain expands more forwards the front of the map and gives this small shrine area where you can see two stone dogs on this map.
