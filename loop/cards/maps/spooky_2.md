## Overview
The second Spooky Map is a modified version of the Dynasty Map. The changes include the water being replaced with fog, being nighttime, the map being rotated 180 degrees, the trees having black bark and no leaves, the pagoda is gone, there are giant thorns blocking the pathways on the left and right sides, the hole has been blocked, preventing units from falling in and there's jack-o-lanterns everywhere.
