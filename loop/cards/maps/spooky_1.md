## Overview
The first Spooky Map is a modified version of Medieval 1. The changes include the water being replaced with fog, being nighttime, the trees having black bark and no leaves, and there's now jack-o-lanterns and candles everywhere. There is a graveyard on the right side of the map. Despite there being no water, units will still take damage and die if placed in the fog.
## Secrets
Click "Expand" for spoiler potential information => Beneath the bridge is a room, which can be reached down a ramp on the left side of the island. Contrary to the Medieval 1 map however, the door appears to be broken, and there is a large hole inside with black tentacles in it. Going down the hole will unlock the fantasy evil map, campaign, and faction. Due to a bug the faction is unlocked at the start of the game.
