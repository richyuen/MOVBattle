## Overview
The Corridor Simulation Map consists of a relatively small area split into two sections by a thick wall and with a narrow corridor between them. Units are meant to be placed on either side, to then fight within the corridor. They have no pathfinding ability to fight enemies not on their level, so if a unit is on the wall and enemies on the normal platform, they will walk off the edge.
