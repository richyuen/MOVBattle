## Overview
Tribal 1 is one of the maps for the Tribal Faction. It is a wide open orange field surrounded by yellow and orange trees, with tree stumps and bent trees blending in. There are a few short hills on the far back and far front area of the map, stretching across from left to right. There is also a campfire on the far right side, accompanied by a dream catcher and a stone dog nearby.
