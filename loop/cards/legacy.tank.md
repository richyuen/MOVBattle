## Characteristics
The Tank is a powerful multi-purpose, diesel powered armored fighting vehicle capable of shooting tank rounds from an incredible distance. On impact, the tank round will create a fiery explosion, blasting through shields and formations. The tank rounds will continue to be fired as long as it is reloaded and a target is within its range. It is incredibly tough and speedy as well. The tank driver will use this advantage to charge and ram through enemy units while using its long turret barrel to bash surrounding targets. On top of the Tank are three Riders, who sit atop the armored vehicle and are vulnerable to enemy fire. They are separate from the Tank and may die on their own, though this does not harm the machine itself. If somehow an enemy unit gets close enough to a Tank Rider, they will attempt to punch them with their fists, though this can prove to be wildly ineffective.
## Appearance
It appears as a modern tank resembling an [https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_Abrams M1 AbramsW] with three riders on it: One is sitting on the back, one sits on top of the turret, and the final one sits on the main cannon.
