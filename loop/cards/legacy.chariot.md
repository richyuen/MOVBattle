## Characteristics
The Chariot is a unit added to TABS during the Pre-Alpha stage of development, but returned to TABS in the Legacy Update in Early Access.
## Appearance
The Chariot is driven by what seems to be a Centurion armed with a gladius, or Roman cavalry sword, a Centurion Shield, and wearing a plumed helmet. It was originally propelled by a stiff wooden-looking horse that would fall apart upon defeat in the Pre-Alpha, but is replaced by a proper horse in the full release. The chariot itself has blades on its wheels. In the Open Alpha, it was redesigned to look like Santa's sleigh because Christmas was near during that time.
