## Characteristics
The Dynamite Thrower is the weakest Unit in the Wild West faction and wields a single piece of dynamite. The dynamite is thrown and explodes on either a timer or a manual detonation, depending if the player possesses it or not.
## Appearance
The Dynamite Thrower appears to wear black boots, a team color skirt with gold lines on it, a rope around its waist, and a team color shirt. It also has a bandanna around its neck, braided hair, and a fancy team color hat. It wields a piece of dynamite that it holds with both hands.
