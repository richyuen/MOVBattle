## Characteristics
Blackbeard is a large unit carrying 2 heavy anchors, accompanied by a long, burning beard. It is one of the strongest units in the Secret Faction, as they can call heavy cannon barrages every few seconds, devastating groups and giant units alike.
## Appearance
Blackbeard wears a long coat and pants, as well as a boot with team-colored lining, as well as a peg leg replacing its left leg, cloth wrapped around its chest, a wide long beard, black hat with firelit strings protruding from it, and a giant anchor strapped onto each hand. Blackbeard has its own unit base in the files which can be accessed via editing them. It appears as a stretched version of the normal wobbler which normally is about twice the size of the average unit.
