## Characteristics
The Hwacha is a firework based artillery of the Dynasty faction that is capable of dealing incredible amount of damage in an area. 60 arrows are shot in a rapid succession in just under 3 seconds. It is operated by the Hwacha Operator.
## Appearance
The Hwacha is a war machine with 60 slots for firework arrows. The Hwacha Operator sits behind the Hwacha and dies with the Hwacha when it is destroyed.
