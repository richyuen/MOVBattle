## Characteristics
The Pike is a Legacy faction Unit that wields a very long, high damage dealing Pike. It's fairly similar to the Sarissa, but has some key differences, one being the most obvious is the length of its weapon, and another one of which being that it loses its pike after one attack then proceeds to slap the opposing team's units.
## Appearance
The Pike wears a fancy Renaissance-themed feathered hat, some white Renaissance armor, a shirt, pants, shoes, and brown hair. They wield a long pike with a hook on one side.
