## Characteristics
The Divine Arbiter holds a magical book that launches homing projectiles of light at an enemy unit; the projectiles themselves do no damage, but they will summon powerful rays of light from above, decimating the targeted enemy as well as anyone near them including friendly units.
## Appearance
The Divine Arbiter uses the wobbler unit base. It wears a hat reminiscent to that of a pope, and has a collar that stands out considerably, like petals on a flower. It has puffy sleeves, and wears two yellow gloves, with both carrying an enchanted book. The bottom part of its robe is rather short, and underneath you will find that it wears a tight pair of grey pants. Curiously, the Divine Arbiter may have forgotten to bring some shoes.
