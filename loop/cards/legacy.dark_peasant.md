## Characteristics
The Dark Peasant is a Legacy ranged Unit in Totally Accurate Battle Simulator. It is equipped with dark hands, encircling it and protecting it from almost every projectile in the game, and blasts of dark magic, capable of dealing massive amounts of damage.
## Appearance
The Dark Peasant uses the generic Wobbler units base, but with red eyes, and a dark and evil auras around its real hands, and has black hands that move around the unit in a sphere, creating a personal shield and protecting it from most incoming projectiles and grabbing its next victim to annihilate. Its clothing consists of a tattered team-colored undershirt, with a patchy stitched-opening jerkin that pinches at elbow/waist level for a more globose silhouette of the late medieval era, black-colored braies (undergarments), and two (presumably) rolled up hosen/chausses, as was commonly practiced in medieval fashion in Europe's' 14th-15th Century, and light colored shoes.
