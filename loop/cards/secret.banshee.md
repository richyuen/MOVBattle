## Characteristics
Banshees are a terrifying foe to face, and are capable of lifting and throwing units off. Its scream is an area attack, and will push every Unit in its radius away, whilst also killing them. The Banshee, however, cannot take extreme amounts of damage. Taking this into account, the Banshee is best left for crowd control over a moderate number of weak units with low HP, and stalling for some time for your army, but not dealing with extremely strong Units, such as the Reaper, King, etc.
