## Characteristics
The Bomb Cannon is a Secret Unit from the Pirate Faction. It is a crowd control unit but can easily self destruct if placed on the wrong spot. It is very powerful but when facing stronger units, it doesn't fair that well as other stronger units. Overall, a risky but blasting way to play.
## Appearance
The Bomb Cannon looks like a bigger normal cannon, except that the gunner has a cutlass in their pores, and there is a small pirate on top of the cannon that wields a cutlass. The small pirate comically raises its cutlass when the cannon is ready to fire. It fires the bomb cannonball, a type of explosive artillery fire capable of dealing a devastating amount of damage to any army.
