## Characteristics
The Flintlock is a sailor armed with two flintlock pistols. The flintlock pistols are close ranged, Flintlocks will have to get close to fire. They throw away the pistols as soon as a shot is fired, and will proceed by punching enemy units.
## Appearance
The Flintlock has rolled-up brown pants with socks underneath, as well as a belt, vest, bandana, shoes, and hair. It wields two flintlock pistols, but also fights bare-handed. The design was slightly changed in the Wild West & Win Conditions Update, removing its hair. This was reverted in the Unit Creator update.
