## Characteristics
The Ice Mage possesses magic that can shoot icicles out of their hands. Those who are hit by these icy projectiles will temporarily get trapped in ice as well as having their hitpoints drained by the second.
## Appearance
The Ice mage uses the Wobbler unit base, they have long, white hair and icy particles around their hands. Their main attire is a long-sleeved undershirt, with the sleeves rolled back, some long overalls that appear to become a skirt, and a sash holding it together. Oddly, even though they wear boots, it only wears shorts, rather than pants.
