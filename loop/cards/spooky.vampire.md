## Characteristics
The Vampire is an extra close melee unit. They suck the enemy's blood, disregarding the exact place that they are drawing blood from or if another Unit is beating them up, and turns into a bat to reach far ones. They are unable to regenerate.
## Appearance
The Vampire looks like a stereotypical vampire: It has fangs, slicked back hair, a beard, a high collar, and a small cape. They wear "classical" garments, like a sleeveless vest, and short pants. If it is far enough from the enemy, it transforms into a bat with the same hairstyle, and flies towards the nearest enemy (Much like the Scarecrow's crows). Once close to the enemy, it transforms back into a vampire.
