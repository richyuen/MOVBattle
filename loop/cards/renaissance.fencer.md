## Characteristics
The Fencer is an agile and quick stepping melee unit of the Renaissance faction. Their primary attack sequentially thrusts forward to attack with its foil then steps back. Fencers also have a dedicated back step, and their AI will use it to try and dodge close-range melee attacks.
## Appearance
The Fencer appears as a wobbler either red or blue and wears a feathered hat and renaissance leather garments, leather boots, long hair and fancy mustache, wielding a Spanish cup-hilt Rapier.
