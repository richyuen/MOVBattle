## Characteristics
The Harvester is a very tough and moderately powerful unit armed with a Scythe. They will use their Scythe to swing and cut at enemy units. Capable of surviving multiple shots, they have very high health and big size but slow movement speed. Considered the poor person's tank, they come with an expensive price, though among the cheap side for other tough units. In combat, the Harvester's Scythe deals moderately high damage but has a slow cooldown, making them very vulnerable between each swing. Nevertheless, their big size and scythe allows them to sweep through groups of fragile units at once, with even enough power to send one into the air. They will also try to keep their distance and dash back from enemy units when they are too close for a good sweep.
## Appearance
The Harvester is a Wobbler of big size wielding a Scythe. They don a simple brown hat and have brown hair with two long braids tied with team-colored bands. They also wear a team-colored ragged tunic with a beige collar that extends to their shoulders along with a pair of dim team-colored ragged pants that is belted with a rope. Their ragged clothing are patched with two light team-colored cloths stitched together with twine. In addition, they wear a pair of brown simple shoes that are tightened with rope.
