## Characteristics
The Brawler (formerly Viking) is a unit that has a small round shield and an axe. They are deadly combatants, with their sharp axe dealing massive damage to units up close and a big round shield for bashing and most importantly protecting them from arrow fire. They also lunge on first contact, in an attempt to get the first hit and an advantage.
## Appearance
The Brawler looks like a viking warrior. In their right hand, the Brawler wields an axe. In its left hand, the Viking has a round wooden shield. It also is wearing a historical Norse helmet, leather boots, cape and a belt. It also has the stereotypical Viking beard.
