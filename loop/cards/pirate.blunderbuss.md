## Characteristics
The Blunderbuss is a heavily armored unit of the Pirate faction. They wear barrel armor that absorbs against almost all projectiles. They wield a blunderbuss that fires with ridiculous recoil that will blow them into the ground.
## Appearance
The Blunderbuss appears to wear armor made from wooden barrel parts, with large straps around their arms, legs, and chest. Hence its name, they also wield a blunderbuss.
