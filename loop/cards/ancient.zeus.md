## Characteristics
Zeus is a unique ranged unit of decent power and HP. They throw Thunderbolts out of both hands that will conduct to nearby units, causing a lightning chain. Zeus can single-handedly deal with weak hordes with their lightning, but may face problems with big and high HP units.
## Appearance
Zeus is a large male-like Wobbler that holds Thunderbolts in each of their hands. They have luscious locks of dirty blond hair and grand beard with hairs that resemble lightning bolts. Zeus also dawns an olive wreath around the sides of their head. They also wear a bright white toga that’s is suspended by their left shoulder. On it is also a golden lightning bolt symbol and is strapped together by a golden belt. Zeus also wears short sandals. The deity will throw their thunderbolts at enemies that can string across multiple units.
