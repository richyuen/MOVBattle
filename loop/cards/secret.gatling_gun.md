## Characteristics
The Gatling Gun is a rapid-firing multi-barred firearm on one axle. It is operated by a Gatling Gun Operator. The Gatling Gun will start by firing at a slow pace, and will gain speed rapidly. It does not reload nor is it required to.
## Appearance
The Gatling Gun Operator appears to have a nice beige shirt and a vest in blue or red with a mustache.
