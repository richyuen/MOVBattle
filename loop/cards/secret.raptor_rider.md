## Characteristics
The Raptor Rider is a tribal warrior riding on a larger, tamed variant of the Raptor. They steer the Raptor to attack their enemies, taking their advantage of their steed's speed to conduct hit-and-run tactics. Armed like the Cavalry Knight but in a primitive form, they mainly use a simple spear to attack and a crude wooden shield to stop incoming projectiles. Compared to their wild breathens, the tamed Raptor is much tougher but also slower. They can still trample units, but has lost their wild instincts in biting. If they were to be put down first whilst in combat, the Raptor Rider will dismount and fight on foot. Conversely, if the Raptor Rider was killed first, the Raptor will expire as well. Like many cavalry units, they have an ability that creates explosions wherever they move, effectively trampling those who are unfortunate enough to get under them.
## Appearance
The rider of the Raptor Rider uses the Wobbler unit base, has short hair, a fur loincloth, and tribal trinkets. They ride a larger than a normal Raptor.
