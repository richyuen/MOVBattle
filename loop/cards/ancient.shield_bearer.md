## Characteristics
The Shield Bearer's main purpose is to block enemy projectiles and attacks, and help its army charge to the frontline, helping the ones behind. If the unit gets close enough to its enemy it also has the ability to attack by shield-bashing the enemy. They are however vulnerable from the behind, and distractions may reveal their Achilles heel to ranged units.
## Appearance
The Shield Bearer is a Wobbler wearing a roman legionnaire helmet and a lorica segmentata (Roman segmented armor). With both hands, it holds an Scutum with a small steel boss (Dome for hand protection) in the center.
