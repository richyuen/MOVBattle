## Characteristics
The Minotaur (known as Asterion in Crete and Θevrumineś in Etruscan) is a tank and good formation disruptor in the Ancient faction. It attacks by charging at its opponents, punching them, and grabbing them. If hit with a projectile, it will charge sooner. It also has a headbutt ability like the Headbutter. It is the "Mini-boss" of the Ancient Faction.
## Appearance
The Minotaur is a brown, anthropomorphic bull with a small tail, white horns, chest hair a brown belt-like sash with a patterned iron belt, and a patterned right shoulder pad. The color of the Minotaur's belt and its shoulder pad changes color depending on what team it's on. The patterns on the belt and shoulder pad change to a lighter color as well.
