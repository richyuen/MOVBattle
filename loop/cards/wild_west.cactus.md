The Cactus is a unique Wild West Faction Unit. It's completely covered in cactus armor, and it shoots spikes at enemy units.

The Cactus is completely covered in cactus armor with some flowers on top. Only parts of its arms, head, and legs are visible. It also has dark brown boots.
