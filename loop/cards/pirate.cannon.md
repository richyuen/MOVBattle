## Characteristics
The Cannon is a gunpowder based artillery unit of the Pirate Faction, it is manned by a Cannon Operator. The Cannon fires the heavy cannonball at enemy units, capable of knocking out even the strongest units with shields.
## Appearance
The Cannon looks like a basic cannon. It has a crosshair on it and two black wheels. The rider wears a white, striped shirt with a bandanna and rolled up pants, as well as boots.
