## Characteristics
The Samurai is a swordsman based on [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Japan#Feudal_Japan FeudalW] [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan Japanese] soldiers. They wield the Katana, a Japanese curved sword, and will strike down enemy units with it. The Samurai is an agile unit, as they are able to deflect projectiles with their katana.
## Appearance
The Samurai appears in either red or blue coloring. In the full release, it is shown to look like a Japanese warrior wearing a Kabuto helmet, Samurai Armor, a Samurai shirt, Samurai wrist Armor, Dynasty pants, Leg Armor and Getas shoes.
