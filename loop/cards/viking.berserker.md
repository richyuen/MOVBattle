## Characteristics
The Berserker is a duel wielding axe-person of the Viking faction, they specialize in closing their gaps with other units, by leaping in great heights to reach enemy units. In combat, they will swing their axes wildly and can chop through lines of units if not stopped.
## Appearance
It wields 2 axes, one in each hand, and wears a horned helmet. It has a beard, and both of their armpits are hairy. When this Unit gets close enough to the enemy units, they leap at them. Like other members of the Viking faction, it wears Landfall's logo on their waist.
