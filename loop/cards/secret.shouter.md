## Characteristics
The Shouter has the ability to give a roaring shout, creating a shockwave that blasts all enemy units away. Every unit within the shockwave will be knocked back with the same power, regardless if they are separate or in a ground. Though powerful, the shockwave itself is harmless and does not cause any damage. Units that are taller or heavier will be more resistant to the shockwave's knockback. Shouts will also be less effective if the unit is right at the Shouter's face. These can be remedied by placing more Shouters to amplify the shockwave. After a shout, the Shouter will try to get close to punch their opponents while they wait for their yelling cooldown. They have a decent pool of hitpoints, but are otherwise weak in combat.
## Appearance
It is a very clear reference a to Skyrim and the iconic dragon shout, Unrelenting Force. It wears a Skyrim-style Nordic iron helmet too, another possible reference, and they wear a Skyrim armor, including an iron vest and the same clothing as the Ullr initially had.
