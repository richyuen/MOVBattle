## Characteristics
The Miner is the second weakest Unit of the Western faction. It wields a pickaxe over its shoulder and has a very fancy mustache. It also deals AoE damage with its pick as a method of offense.
## Appearance
The Miner appears to have team colored overalls with the flap open showing where the buttocks would be, team color gloves, a black mustache, a fancy team color hat, and black boots. It also has a pickaxe it holds over its shoulder.
