## Characteristics
The Ninja (Shinobi) throws powerful shurikens at enemies. They don't throw nearly as many shurikens as their greater counterpart, the Sensei, but they make up for it with their lower price.
## Appearance
The ninja is a Wobbler wearing colored hood and robes, concealing most of its body, and which are corresponding to its team's color, while also holding a shuriken on each hand. Only the eyes are visible.
