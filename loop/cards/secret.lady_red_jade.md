## Characteristics
Lady Red Jade is a melee Unit that attacks by swinging their weapon in very wide arcs, sometimes even in complete circles. They will do this consecutively, even using spinning kicks to attack. The Lady Red Jade does not spin as continuously as the Mace Spinner does, but its attacks are still swift. Lady Red Jade is not fragile, and will need many attacks to take this warrior down. As of the Unit Creator update, Lady Red Jade now has a new ability named 'Tornado Swipe', which allows them to make a small tornado every 15 seconds, which ragdolls units around the air and deals low damage. Lady Red Jade can also deflect projectiles now.
## Appearance
Lady Red Jade appears as a substantially tall wobbler, donning a decorated dress with elbow length sleeves, a knee length skirt, and boots. Lady Red Jade also wears a seeming hairpiece, along with wielding their mighty Guandao.
