## Characteristics
The Teacher is a skillful swordsman with the ability to parry or dodge incoming melee attacks. They will always parry when possible, and dodge if it is unfeasible or risky to only parry. Certain weapons are much easier to parry before the Teacher gets overwhelmed. As skillful as the Teacher is, some parryable attacks may be too fast or well-aimed to ward off completely. Their reaction speed may also prove slow at times to dodge incoming attacks. Unlike other units who charge blindly, the Teacher will maintain a good distance, avoid their adversary's attack and strike when the opportunity arises. This approach plays it safe, but makes the Teacher unable to fight back effectively if their adversary's weapons are unparryable, undodgeable or simply has a long attack range.
## Appearance
This unit is a Wobbler with a long renaissance looking hair with a triangular beard (Similar to portraits of Shakespeare). They wear renaissance garments including a jacket with a large square jabot and dark pants with little black shoes. They wield a sword similar to that of a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spadroon Spadroon], a fancy one handed blade.
