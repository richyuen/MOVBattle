## Characteristics
The Harpooner is a rather interesting unit in which they are armed with a harpoon gun, a weapon that shoots harpoons at enemy units. The Harpooner will then reel in the enemy unit, and kicking them once in melee range. They are rather tough, and are dangerous as they pick on units one by one.
## Appearance
This unit wears an open coat, undershirt, rolled-up beige pants, brown shoes, and a brown tricorn hat. It also has a fairly thick beard. The Harpooner wields a portable harpoon gun that has a sword-bayonet under the barrel.
