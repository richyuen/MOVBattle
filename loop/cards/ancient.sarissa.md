## Characteristics
The Sarissa (Formerly known as the Spearman) is an effective melee unit of the Ancient faction. The Sarissa allows units to stab the enemy from a safer distance, and allows for dense formations and still be offensively efficient. They will also back up to maintain spearing distance. However, they are very lightly armored, and are vulnerable to any ranged attacks.
## Appearance
The Sarissa is a Wobbler of either red or blue color that wears a Macedonian helmet while holding a long spear. It also has a cape and a big brown belt around its hips, holding what seems to be an [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acinaces Acinaces] dagger.
