## Characteristics
The Witch is a broom riding unit with magical attacks. They throw spells that make those who were hit grow mushrooms, and making the units hit start attacking their allies whilst ignoring the real enemies, all while they are poisoned to death. They will also summon two clones side by side, each two will throw mushroom spells, though they do fade after 8 seconds. So overall, if you want a good amount of support on your side, and a great distraction that can fight, then this Unit may be a good choice for your army.
## Appearance
The Witch, contradicting to many stereotypical interpretations, looks like a Stiffy riding a floating broom, with mushrooms growing out of it and the units attire. It appears to wear a tall hat, and a tattered robe with trousers that have been ripped to its femurs, and wields a Witch Staff.
