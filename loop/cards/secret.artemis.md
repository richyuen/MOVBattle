## Characteristics
Artemis is a powerful ranged unit that fires several arrows from their bow at a time. Occasionally they will charge their attack for a moment and fire a large knock-back arrow, which sends all units in a line in front of them flying.
## Appearance
Artemis is a Unit sporting a Greek light dress and a quiver. Unlike its mythological counterpart, they fire a cluster of five arrows from its bow at once.
