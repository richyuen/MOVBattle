## Characteristics
The Bomb On A Stick is a suicidal unit, carrying a bomb tied to a stick, they will charge the enemy and smack them with the bomb, creating a big blast that inevitably kills themselves along with the enemy unit.
## Appearance
The Bomb on a Stick has shaggy ginger hair wrapped into a long braid on their back, a team colored headband and hair tie wrapped around the top of their head and near the end of the braid respectively. They wear a beige, baggy shirt with a few brown strands at the neck and elbow length, seemingly rolled up sleeves. A team colored vest is worn and wrapped over it, brown buttons lining the right side of it. They wear very baggy team colored pants with a singular team colored cloth patching up the outer side of the left leg, stitched onto the pants with white threads. Lastly, they wear team colored shoes with no socks, each one donning a large gray buckle. In both of their hands they wield their weapon with the same name as them, an always lit bomb strapped to a long stick.
