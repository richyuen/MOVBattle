## Characteristics
The Valkyrie is a flying unit duel-wielding swords. Upon nearing an enemy unit, they will swoop down and attack swiftly. Their flying ability puts them in an advantage of the high ground, and can wreak havoc on formations trying to kill them. While fast and agile when engaged, they fly very slowly when unengaged, and can be shot down easily.
## Appearance
The Valkyrie is a Wobbler with Viking armor and a large pair of wings on the back. It carries two Swords and hovers above the ground.
