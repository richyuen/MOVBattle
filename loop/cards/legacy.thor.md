## Characteristics
Thor is a Legacy Faction Unit who wields its famous hammer, Mjölnir, and uses various lightning-based attacks. It is the Norse God of Thunder, after all.
## Appearance
Thor uses the Wobbler unit base. Thor wears a metal headband, with two wings on it. Thor also has a short sleeved woolen coat, which reveals quite a bit of their chest and abdomen, and has a stylish Viking belt, albeit without the Landfall logo, and knee length pants, topped off with some fine leathery boots. Thor's hammer, Mjölnir, appears to be a mallet, with a black tether indicating the grip, and a simple hammer head with a glowing circle on the left and right centers of the hammer head, surrounded by lightning bolts. However, there is only a lightning bolt on the left on the other side of Mjölnir.
