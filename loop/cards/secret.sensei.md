## Characteristics
The Sensei is a Secret ranged unit in Totally Accurate Battle Simulator. It will throw up to 3 Sensei Shurikens per hand, each one with low damage but a lot of knockback, and will deliver these out as faster than the usual Ninja. Another thing is that if a Unit gets close to it (unless its an ally) then it will either dash backwards, or maybe use its Shuriken Tempest, spitting out ninja stars in 3 rows and deflecting around 50 projectiles for a limited time. However, it's rather costly, and despite being so, they're still a glass cannon, so keep them protected and it'll protect you and your team. But if its your opponent, it's recommended you use something like the Cannon, or even an Archer far away, as while it may have quite the range, they can't reach that far, so use this and its low HP to your advantage.
## Appearance
Sensei wear some sort of cloth armor and a helmet. It wears spiked armor on their forearms and holds one shuriken per hand that are significantly different from a normal Ninja's, spinning when thrown and overall having far more points.
