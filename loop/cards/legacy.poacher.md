## Characteristics
The Poacher is a basic rapid-fire ranged Unit from the Legacy faction. They have a rather short range, but a very high fire rate.
## Appearance
It wears a wide-brimmed hat and a sleeveless coat trimmed with fur. It also has a poacher's glove, a quiver, fur boots, a belt, and knee caps. And white long hair with braids. It wields a short bow.
