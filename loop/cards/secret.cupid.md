## Characteristics
The Cupid is a small flying Wobbler that shoots love arrows at their enemies. Units who are shot with this arrow will lose interest in attacking their enemies for 10 seconds and instead draw their attention toward their allies. If an ally gets too close, they will kiss them relentlessly, draining their hitpoints until the effects wear off or the kisser is dead. However, lovestruck units will usually maintain social distance from their allies, depending on their weapon range. If there is no other allies on the map, they will continue walking and ignore Cupid. As Cupid's cooldown is much shorter than the effect's duration, they can easily keep a single unit dazed in love until they die from the arrow or the miniscule health drain from the effect.
## Appearance
The unit looks like Cupid as it has blonde hair, wings to make them fly, wears a diaper, and carries a heart bow with heart arrows.
