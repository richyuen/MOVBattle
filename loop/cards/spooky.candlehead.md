## Characteristics
The Candlehead is a mystical ranged unit of the Spooky Faction. They throw spirit fire at medium range, pushing enemies up into the air.
## Appearance
The Candlehead wears a robe and has flaming cierges on its head and hands, with half-long leggings with an opening for its "toes".
