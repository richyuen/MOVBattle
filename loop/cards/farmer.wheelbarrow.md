## Characteristics
The Wheelbarrow is an especially tough and and moderately powerful unit that consists of three sub-units: the Traveller, the Apple Thrower and the Farmer. The Traveller is the driver of the Wheelbarrow, steering the cart with Apple Thrower and Farmer riding on top. It will ram and run over enemy units whilst the Apple Thrower throws apples and the Farmer stabs any enemy units up close. The cart is a unit in itself and can be destroyed; that said, it has an especially high health and extremely fast speed, capable of outrunning most units in the game. Coming with a really expensive but among the side of the price, they are considered the poor person's cavalry. In combat, the Wheelbarrow running over units deal extremely low damage; however, combining the Farmer and the Apple Thrower's attacks makes it capable to deal moderately high damage.
## Appearance
The Wheelbarrow is a vehicle with a single wheel at the front with two supporting legs and two handles at the rear. It has a tray that is made out of wooden planks bolted together to form a rectangular shape; the tray is crudely painted with the team's color and filled with hay. The handles, legs and braces are also made out of wood, bolted to the tray. The wheel is made out of a cut log, with its center bolted to the brace. Sitting on the Wheelbarrow is two riders, the Farmer at the front and the Apple Thrower at behind. At the rear end of it is the Traveller, grabbing its two handles.
