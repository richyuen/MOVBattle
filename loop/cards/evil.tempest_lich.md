## Characteristics
The Tempest Lich wields a stave that will cast a storm on enemy units. The clouds strikes powerful lightning bolts upon anyone underneath the cloud. However, the only problem is just how fragile it is, as a simple club is enough to knock it out cold. Keeping this in mind, just like the Sensei, use this at the back of your army, possibly in an inaccessible place, so it won't be as vulnerable.
## Appearance
The Tempest Lich appears with no head instead having black appendages (similar to the Reaper) emerging from the torso and place where the head should be. The Tempest Lich also carries a staff with a crude design on the top and the symbol of a lightning bolt on the bottom.
