## Characteristics
Ullr (Pronounced Ooler) is a Unit in the Secret Faction. It is a specialized ranged unit, as it attacks by throwing its Double-Headed axes at opposing units, freezing them like the Ice Archer, except for Ullr's explosion and an additional AoE ice effect.
## Appearance
Ullr used to appear as a red or blue wobbler with short Viking pants with a Viking helmet, as well as a medium-sized beard and a mustache. It has worn boots with ties around them, and a landfall belt with a Viking cape and vest. Now Ullr wears a shirt with a belt and jacket on it and long pants with boots. It has a green beard and hair, a fur cape, and a snow cap with mini axes on it.
