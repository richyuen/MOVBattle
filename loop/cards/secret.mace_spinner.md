## Characteristics
The Mace Spinner is a tough and durable unit wielding two maces as their weapons. Instead of swinging the maces normally, they attack by spinning their whole body and extending their arms, turning themselves into a blender of sorts.
## Appearance
The Mace Spinner looks like a Red or Blue Wobbler dual wielding maces. It is dons plate-armor boots, a chest-plate, and a helmet very similar to the one of a Squire but a lot less cubic and more round with no holes.
