## Characteristics
The Knight is an armored and formidable Medieval unit. They wield an [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knightly_sword Arming Swordw] and a large shield that protects it from most attacks. Upon at close range, the Knight will charge in and attack, utilizing both sword and shield to hack and batter enemy units in a hasty attack.
## Appearance
The Knight is a tall Wobbler wearing plate armor covering most of its body, a team colored cape, and an open armet helmet. It uses a sword and a rather large shield with a white cross painted on.
